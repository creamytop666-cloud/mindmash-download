Mind Mash Account Deletion

A Word and Math Game

For parents and legal guardians

Request Account Deletion

Mind Mash is an educational application for preschool learners supported by Golden Knowledge Learning Center. A parent or legal guardian can request deletion of their Mind Mash account and associated learner information without reinstalling or signing in to the app.

How to Submit a Request

  1. Email mindmash.support@gmail.com with the subject Mind Mash Account Deletion Request.
  2. Include the Google email address used for Mind Mash registration and the learner name entered in the app.
  3. State that you are the parent or legal guardian requesting account deletion.
  4. Wait for the support team to respond with appropriate steps to verify your authority before the account is archived.
Send Deletion Request via Gmail

Privacy reminder: Do not send your Google password, sign-in codes, or identification documents in your initial email. The button opens Gmail in a new browser tab. Sign in to Gmail if prompted, then review and send the message yourself. If Gmail is unavailable, manually email mindmash.support@gmail.com with the subject Mind Mash Account Deletion Request.

What Happens to Your Account and Data?

After the request is verified, an authorized administrator archives the learner account. Under Mind Mash's current archive policy, archived accounts are automatically permanently deleted 30 days after the archival date.

This deletion includes the learner account and related personal information and educational records, including saved answers, scores, stars, lesson progress, and pretest and posttest results.

Timing: The 30-day period begins on the date the account is archived, not the date the initial email is sent. Requests should be reviewed and processed without unnecessary delay.

What Data Is Retained?

While an account remains active, Mind Mash retains the account and learning information needed to provide its educational functions. Once the account is archived and the 30-day period ends, the account and associated learner records are permanently deleted under the stated policy. Any legally required exceptions, if applicable, should be communicated to the requester.
