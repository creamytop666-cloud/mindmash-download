Mind Mash Privacy Policy

A Word and Math Game

Last updated: November 10, 2026

Introduction

Mind Mash: A Word and Math Game is an educational application for young children. A parent or legal guardian should assist with registration and supervise the child’s use of the app. Golden Knowledge Learning Center operates the educational service, with technical support from the Mind Mash development team.

1. INFORMATION WE PROCESS

Mind Mash processes the Google account identifier (UID) and email address used by the parent or guardian for sign-in; the learner’s name, age, and gender; and educational information such as answers, lesson progress, scores, stars, pretest and posttest results, and earned rewards.

2. HOW WE USE INFORMATION

We use this information to authenticate accounts, identify learner profiles, save and restore educational progress, display scores and achievements, and support the app’s educational and administrative functions.

3. GOOGLE SIGN-IN AND ADULT SUPERVISION

Registration uses a parent or guardian’s Google account. Parents or guardians should supervise children while using Mind Mash. Google Sign-In is used for authentication; the app’s account and progress features depend on the information described above.

4. PARENT OR GUARDIAN PROGRESS VIEW

Parents or guardians can access the View Children Progress feature at https://mindmash-admin-production.up.railway.app/ by entering the Google email address used during registration and the learner name recorded in the app. This feature displays the learner’s educational progress. The website’s progress lookup should be used only by authorized parents or guardians.

5. STORAGE, ACCESS, AND SECURITY

Account and educational records are stored using the application’s backend and database services. Authorized Golden Knowledge Learning Center personnel and the Mind Mash development team may access information as needed to operate, maintain, and support the service. Access should be limited to authorized purposes and protected by appropriate security measures.

6. SHARING OF INFORMATION

Mind Mash does not sell learner personal information. Information is processed through services needed to provide the app, including Google Sign-In, application hosting, and database hosting. Information may also be disclosed when required by applicable law.

7. DATA RETENTION

Learner account information and educational records are retained while the account remains active and the information is needed to provide the educational service. There is no separate automatic one-year deletion schedule for active accounts.

8. ACCOUNT AND DATA DELETION

A parent or legal guardian may request deletion by emailing mindmash.support@gmail.com. After verifying the requester’s authority, an authorized administrator archives the learner account. The existing system automatically and permanently deletes the archived account and its related personal information and learning records 30 days after the archival date. The 30-day period begins on the date of archival, not on the date the email request is sent. Requests should be reviewed and processed without unnecessary delay.

9. ACCESS AND CORRECTION

Parents or legal guardians may contact mindmash.support@gmail.com to request access to or correction of learner information. The team may take reasonable steps to verify that the requester is authorized.

10. POLICY UPDATES

This policy may be updated when Mind Mash features or data practices change. The current public version is available at https://mindmash-download.pages.dev/privacy-policy.html.

11. CONTACT

For privacy questions and requests, contact mindmash.support@gmail.com. Educational service: Golden Knowledge Learning Center; technical support: Mind Mash development team.
